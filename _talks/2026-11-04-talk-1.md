Co-presented with Kirstin Williamson on behalf of Alyson Fox, Director of Research Funding at Wellcome, during the Agentic AI and the Future of Funding workshop. The presentation explored the increasing use of LLMs in the grant application process, the detection of LLM use using BERT based models, broader funding policy implications, and how we are exploring the use of LLMs and agentic systems within the funding management process.
