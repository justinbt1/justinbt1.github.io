Hello! I lead the Data Science Team and profession within Data & Digital at the [Wellcome Trust](https://wellcome.org/what-we-do). Where we are working to help understand Wellcome’s medical research funding portfolio and its impacts. 

Currently I am focused on applying machine learning, graph analysis and natural language processing (NLP) techniques to develop metrics and measures for assessing the impact of our funding portfolio. If you are interested, you can follow our work on the [Wellcome Data blog](https://medium.com/wellcome-data).

Originally graduating with a BSc in Geology & Petroleum Geology from the [University of Aberdeen](https://www.abdn.ac.uk/geosciences/departments/geology/), I spent my early career working in a number of roles across the Oil and Gas industry. During this time I gained experience working with data, particularly with document understanding, NLP and PySpark, eventually becoming lead Data Scientist heading up a research and development team. 

I've also completed an MSc in Data Science from [Birkbeck, University of London](https://www.dcs.bbk.ac.uk), with my [dissertation](https://github.com/justinbt1/Multimodal-Document-Classification) focusing on developing a multimodal approach for combining text and page images to classify complex multi-page documents within the [North Sea Transition Authority’s](https://www.nstauthority.co.uk) [National Data Repository](https://ndr.nstauthority.co.uk).

I am interested in and always happy to talk about the use of AI and machine learning in research funding, the use of reinforcement learning to simulate funding choices, ethics and best practises, and data science for public good.
